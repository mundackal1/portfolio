01About

Built from the front desk up.

I hosted guests at my family's homestay, ran a 34-room hotel for eight years, and managed 380+ hotel partners at Cleartrip.

That's where I learned how rates, inventory and reservations really break, and how much small businesses lose to tools that don't fit them. Now I build those tools myself: I talk to the people who'll use them, define the workflow, and ship web and mobile products with modern frameworks and AI-assisted development.

Today I'm Product & Business Development Manager at Akshaya Group, where I built Room IQ, Chit IQ and Fin IQ.
